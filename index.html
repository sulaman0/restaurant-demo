Demo for your restaurant

An AI host that answers guests while you cook

Guests ask the same questions every evening: are you open, is it spicy, can I book for six, can I pick up at 19:00. This assistant answers them on your website, WhatsApp or Instagram, in Danish and English, day and night.

  • 1
    Books tablesTakes the booking and sends it to you or your booking system right away. No more missed calls during the rush.
  • 2
    Takes takeaway orders directlyOrders come to you instead of through Wolt or Just Eat, so you keep the commission.
  • 3
    Knows your menuAnswers about spice level, vegetarian dishes, allergens and halal from your real menu.
  • 4
    Speaks Danish and EnglishTourists and locals both get answers in their own language.

Try it: tap a button in the chat or type a question like "is the lamb spicy?" or "bord til 4 i morgen".
